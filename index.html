01 · Business question

Every support contact is a signal.

Ops leadership wanted to cut wait times and repeat contacts without simply hiring more agents. I framed the work around four questions:

01

When is demand highest?

Volume by month, weekday and hour, so staffing follows real demand.

02

How are clients switching channels?

The move from phone and branch toward chat and the mobile app.

03

Which requests cause the most friction?

Resolution time, escalations and repeat contacts by request type.

04

What drives satisfaction (CSAT)?

Measuring how response speed, resolution time and escalation relate to CSAT.

1Extract tickets from the CRM and survey tool
2Clean: dedupe, fix timezones, cap outliers

4EDA plus correlation-based driver analysis
5Size the impact of each action, then present
-- repeat-contact rate by category
SELECT category,
  COUNT(*) AS tickets,
  AVG(repeat_contact::int) AS repeat_rate,
  PERCENTILE_CONT(0.5)
    WITHIN GROUP (ORDER BY resolution_hrs)
FROM service.tickets
GROUP BY category
ORDER BY repeat_rate DESC;
